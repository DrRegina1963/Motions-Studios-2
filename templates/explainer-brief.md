## Tips from the first video

- Step headlines read best at 1–4 words; step lines at about 10 words or fewer.
- 3 steps get about 2.5 seconds each in 15 seconds; 4 steps get about 2 seconds each. More steps → make the video longer.
- 1920x1080 suits YouTube and websites. For Instagram, consider 1080x1350 (feed) or 1080x1920 (Reels/Stories).
- Long titles and calls to action are split over two lines, using your exact words.
